# AASB disclosure pointers

These are questions, not opinions. Open the current compilation on https://standards.aasb.gov.au/ before you cite a paragraph. On 13 November 2026 the portal still showed compilation pages whose stated end dates had passed or were close (AASB 15's page said periods beginning before 1 July 2026). Treat every paragraph number as unchecked until you have the current PDF open.

Do not write "complies with AASB X" in the draft. Write "question for the reporting team" and the evidence they point to.

| Standard | What to open | Question for this pack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AASB 134 Interim Financial Reporting | Compilation page read on 13 November 2026: https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-134-dec-2022 and the PDF https://www.aasb.gov.au/admin/file/content105/c9/AASB134_08-15_COMPdec22_01-23.pdf | Half year only. The December 2022 compilation, paragraph 8, lists a condensed statement of financial position, profit or loss and other comprehensive income, changes in equity, cash flows, and selected explanatory notes. Confirm that compilation is still operative. Ask which events and transactions since the annual report are disclosed, and which recognition questions (tax, impairment, leases) the auditor wants tested at the interim date. |
| AASB 101 Presentation of Financial Statements | https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-101-dec-2022-0 | The AASB 134 compilation defines a complete set of financial statements by reference to AASB 101. If the half-year report is a complete set rather than condensed, say so. For the annual report, ask whether the primary statements, comparatives, and material accounting policy information match the current AASB 101. |
| AASB 8 Operating Segments | Open the current compilation from the AASB portal. No paragraph number is cited here. | If the entity has segments in the annual report, ask whether the interim report updates segment revenue, profit, and assets in the way the current standard and AASB 134 require. Do not invent a segment. |
| AASB 15 Revenue from Contracts with Customers | https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-15-dec-2022 | Ask where revenue from ordinary activities in Appendix item 2.1 is built from, and which disaggregation the current standard requires in the annual report and, through AASB 134, in the half year. |
| AASB 16 Leases | Open the current compilation from the AASB portal. No paragraph number is cited here. | Ask what changed in lease liabilities, right-of-use assets, and lease expense, and whether any modification or new lease is material enough for the interim notes. |
| AASB 136 Impairment of Assets | PDF opened on 13 November 2026: https://www.aasb.gov.au/admin/file/content105/c9/aasb136_08-15.pdf The objective on that PDF is that assets are not carried above recoverable amount. Confirm it is the current compilation. | Ask whether indicators exist at the reporting date, which cash-generating units were tested, and whether an impairment loss or reversal is in the numbers that go to item 2.2. |
| AASB 107 Statement of Cash Flows | Cited by Listing Rule 4.2C.4 | If the cash flow statement is condensed, confirm it still reports each significant form of cash flow as that rule requires. |
