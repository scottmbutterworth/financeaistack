# ASX half-year and full-year reporting

Use this skill to plan the pack and draft the working papers. It does not lodge anything, and it does not decide what is material.

Licence: MIT. The licence file is `LICENCE.md` in this folder.

## When to use

- You are preparing an Appendix 4D (half year) or an Appendix 4E (preliminary final report) for an entity listed on ASX.
- You need a timetable counted back from the ASX deadline, a checklist of the form items, and a hand-off list for the directors and the auditor.
- You want the results announcement and the investor presentation checked against the same numbers.

Do not use it for a mining exploration entity or an oil and gas exploration entity until you have read the different deadline in Listing Rule 4.2B. Do not use it as a substitute for the current Appendix 4D or 4E PDF.

## What this skill will not do

- It will not invent a profit, a dividend, a date, or a percentage.
- It will not sign a directors' declaration or an auditor's report.
- It will not tell you that information is, or is not, market sensitive. That is a Listing Rule 3.1 judgement for the entity.

## Inputs you need

Ask for these before you draft. If one is missing, say so and leave the related cells blank.

- Legal name, ASX code, and whether the entity is a company or a trust.
- Balance date, and whether this pack is the half year or the full year.
- Whether the entity is a mining exploration entity or an oil and gas exploration entity. If the user does not know, stop the timetable and tell them to confirm it. The deadline changes.
- The reporting period and the previous corresponding period.
- Trial balance or draft financial statements, with the source file name and the date of the extract.
- Draft dividend or a clear statement that none is proposed, including the amount per security, the franked amount per security, and the record date, if those have been decided.
- Auditor status: review underway, audit underway, or not yet started. Do not describe accounts as reviewed or audited unless the user has given you the report.
- The current Appendix 4D or 4E form, or confirmation that you should work from the PDFs linked below and flag every item you cannot see on a current form.
- Board and audit-committee dates, and the date the market announcements office must have the document.

## Deadlines you may use, and only these

Count back from the deadline that applies. Put the period end, the deadline, and the source rule on the timetable. See `timetable.md`.

**Half year, entities that are not mining exploration or oil and gas exploration entities.** Listing Rule 4.2A requires the half-year documents, including the Appendix 4D information in rule 4.2A.3. Listing Rule 4.2B says give them immediately they are ready, and no later than lodgement of the accounts with ASIC (or the home regulator), and in any event no later than two months after the end of the accounting period.

**Half year, mining exploration and oil and gas exploration entities.** Listing Rule 4.2B sets the outside date at 75 days after the end of the accounting period. Rule 4.2A.3 says the Appendix 4D information is not required for those entities. Do not put a two-month 4D date on their timetable.

**Preliminary final report.** Listing Rule 4.3A requires the Appendix 4E information except for a mining exploration entity or an oil and gas exploration entity. Listing Rule 4.3B says give it immediately it is ready, no later than lodgement of the accounts with ASIC or the home regulator, and in any event no later than two months after the end of the accounting period. Compliance Update 12/25 (31 October 2025) says the same thing in its discussion of Listing Rule 4.3B.

**Annual documents.** Listing Rule 4.5.1 requires an Australian disclosing entity to give ASX the documents it must lodge with ASIC under section 319 of the Corporations Act when it lodges them, and no later than three months after the end of the accounting period, together with any concise report. Compliance Update 12/25 says the Listing Rule 4.5 documents are due no later than three months after the period end, which is later than the two-month Appendix 4E date.

**Sustainability report.** Compliance Update 12/25 says the Appendix 4E is not there to pull statutory sustainability-report content forward. That content generally waits for the Listing Rule 4.5 date unless Listing Rule 3.1 requires it earlier. Read the current rule before you write this into a board paper. The ASX listings compliance activities report for the following quarter describes Compliance Update 13/25 (19 December 2025) as confirming that a change to Listing Rule 17.5, so that a late annual sustainability report would not by itself cause mandatory suspension, would take effect on 16 January 2026. The report is at https://www.asx.com.au/content/dam/asx/about/asx-supervision/compliance-activity-reporting/2025/asx-listings-compliance-activities-report-q4-cy25.pdf. This skill did not re-read the amended text of rule 17.5. Confirm it before you describe the suspension consequence.

**If you miss the date.** Module 2 of the ASX course says that if an entity fails to give ASX a periodic report covered by that module by the due date, ASX will automatically suspend quotation before market open on the next trading day, without further warning, under Listing Rule 17.5, and that ASX will not waive that rule. Read the current rule 17.5. Do not invent a clock time for the market announcements office. A past compliance update used an example close of 8:30pm AEDT for one February date. That is not a standing rule in this skill. Confirm the current office hours for the due date you are using.

**Listing Rule 3.1 still runs.** The notes to Listing Rules 4.2B and 4.3B say that information which is market sensitive under rule 3.1 must be given immediately, even if it will also sit in the periodic report. Open Guidance Note 8 before you discuss the 3.1A carve-out. Do not apply the carve-out yourself.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the entity category and the period. Write the outside ASX date from the rule above, then the ASIC lodgement date if the user has one, then the board meeting. The ASX date is the earlier of "when the accounts are lodged" and the two-month or 75-day cap, and the pack should be ready before either.
2. Build the timetable in `timetable.md`. Work backwards: auditor sign-off, audit committee, board, announcement drafting freeze, numbers freeze. Leave a row for "Listing Rule 3.1 check on the morning of release".
3. Walk the current Appendix 4D or 4E from the PDF, item by item, using `appendix-checklist.md`. For 4D, the form read on 13 November 2026 (introduced 01/01/03, amended 17/12/10 and 01/01/12) asks for items 1 to 9, including "Results for announcement to the market" at items 2.1 to 2.6. For 4E, the same reading confirmed item 1 and items 2.1 to 2.6. Complete every later item from the current PDF. If you cannot see the PDF, list the item as "not checked against the current form".
4. Apply the lodgement shape from Module 2 and Listing Rules 4.2C and 4.3C. One PDF. Results for announcement to the market at the front. Half-year cover page states that the information should be read with the most recent annual financial report (rule 4.2C.2). Preliminary final report is identified as given under rule 4.3A (rule 4.3C.1). Dividend amount and record date are in the announcement. Appendix 3A.1 is a separate online form after the results. Media releases and investor presentations are a later announcement, not inside the 4D or 4E PDF. Module 2 points to section 16 of Guidance Note 14.
5. Draft the disclosure pointers in `disclosure-pointers.md`. These are questions for the reporting team and the auditor, not conclusions.
6. Write the hand-off. Half-year accounts given under the Australian limb of rule 4.2A.1 are the documents a disclosing entity lodges with ASIC under section 320. Do not badge them as reviewed or audited unless the report is in the file. Full-year Appendix 4E may include audited or unaudited accounts (Module 2). If they are unaudited, flag Listing Rule 4.3D: once the entity is aware of circumstances likely to materially affect the preliminary final report, it must immediately explain the circumstances and the expected effect. Module 2 gives the audit adjustment as the typical case.
7. For the annual directors' declaration, use the current Corporations Act sections 295 and 295A. Section 295A applies where the company, disclosing entity, or registered scheme is listed. The directors' declaration under subsection 295(4) is made only after each person who performs a chief executive function or a chief financial officer function has given the written, signed declaration in subsection 295A(2). That declaration covers the section 286 records, compliance with accounting standards, the true and fair view (section 297), the consolidated entity disclosure statement required by subsection 295(3A), and any prescribed matters. The directors' declaration itself includes the solvency opinion and, if listed, that the section 295A declarations were given. Do not copy the annual section 295A wording onto the half-year pack. Open the current half-year provisions in Chapter 2M and follow those.
8. Run `consistency-checks.md` across the Appendix, the financial report, the media release, and the investor presentation. Every figure in the presentation must trace to a number in the pack or be deleted.
9. Stop. Hand the draft to the CFO, the auditor, and the company secretary. Do not lodge it.

## Checks before you finish

- Every date in the timetable cites Listing Rule 4.2B, 4.3B, or 4.5, or is labelled as an internal working date.
- Mining exploration and oil and gas exploration entities are not on the two-month Appendix 4D path.
- No figure appears that was not in an input file. Percentages show the numerator and the denominator.
- "Reviewed" or "audited" appears only when the user supplied the report.
- The results-for-announcement block is at the front, and the presentation is not inside the 4D or 4E PDF.
- Dividend amount, franked amount, and record date match the Appendix 3A.1 draft, or the draft says no dividend is proposed.
- Section 295A is used for the annual listed-entity declaration only.
- Open items are listed as open. Nothing is marked complete to make the pack look finished.
