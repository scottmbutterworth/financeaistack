# Example reconciliation

EXAMPLE DATA. Not a real employer and not a real pay run.

Example Pty Ltd. March 2026 pay run, one pay date of 15 March 2026. That date is before 1 July 2026, so the example uses the quarterly super rule and the 12 per cent rate read from the ATO page for 2025–26.

| Item | Example payroll | Example ledger | Difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gross wages | 10,000 | 10,000 | 0 |
| PAYG withheld | 2,000 | 2,000 | 0 |
| Super at 12 per cent of 10,000 | 1,200 | 1,000 | 200 |

The 200 difference is the exception in this example. It is not a conclusion about why the ledger is short. A live file needs the user's export before anyone writes a cause.

Payday Super does not apply to this example pay date. A pay date of 15 July 2026 would be tested under Payday Super, including the 7 business day receipt rule, after the ATO page is re-read.
