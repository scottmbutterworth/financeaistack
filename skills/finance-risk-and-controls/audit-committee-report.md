## Recommendation 7, if this is the annual review

Complete this only when the user is preparing the annual disclosure. The 4th edition asks the board or a committee to review the framework at least annually and to disclose whether the review took place (Recommendation 7.2). This paper can record the finance work that was put in front of the committee. It cannot say the review occurred unless the minutes say it occurred.
